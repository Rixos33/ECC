## Description (YouTube Studio -> Customisation -> Basic info)
Big questions. Short answers.

Why can't you tickle yourself? Why does your phone's map need Einstein? What actually happens in your brain
on a drug, and why?

Mr. Shorts is a small owl in glasses and red shorts who can't leave a good question alone. Each video takes
one question about science or the mind and answers it in about a minute: animated, sourced, and honest
about what researchers don't know yet. Every Short ends with the question the next one answers.

New Shorts every week. Sources for every claim are in the video descriptions.

## Short version (about 150 characters, for search results)
One-minute animated answers to big questions about science and the mind, narrated by a human and hosted by an owl in shorts.
