# YouTube Channel Growth

Orchestrates existing ECC skills and agents into one channel-building workflow. No growth is guaranteed; treat targets as hypotheses to test.

## When to Activate

- starting a YouTube channel or choosing a niche
- planning video formats, hooks, titles, and thumbnails
- building a production workflow or upload schedule
- reviewing channel analytics
- preparing for YouTube Partner Program (YPP) monetization

## How It Works

Gather inputs first: niche interests, target audience, available time per week, equipment, budget, current subscribers/watch hours, and goals. Ask if missing. Verify current YPP thresholds on YouTube's official help pages; they change.

### 1. Niche and Positioning
- Use `market-research` / `deep-research` to assess demand, search volume, competition, and monetization potential.
- Use `competitive-platform-analysis` to benchmark 5-10 channels.
- Use `brand-discovery` / `brand-voice` to define positioning and voice.
- Output: one-sentence channel promise, 3-4 content pillars, target viewer.

### 2. Video Concepts
- Use `content-engine` for scripts, hooks, and repurposing (Shorts, clips).
- Use the `seo-specialist` agent for keywords, titles, and descriptions.
- For each concept: title options, thumbnail idea, first-30-seconds hook, retention beats, one clear payoff.

### 3. Production System
- Use `video-editing` (FFmpeg, Remotion, voiceover tools) for the pipeline: research → script → record → edit → thumbnail → publish.
- Batch work weekly; keep a repeatable checklist per video.

### 4. Growth and Analytics Loop
Track per video: impressions, CTR, average view duration, retention graph, returning viewers, subscriber conversion, traffic sources.
- Low CTR → rework title/thumbnail.
- Early retention drop → rework the hook.
- Mid-video drop → tighten pacing/structure.
- Record one learning per video and apply it to the next.

### 5. Monetization
Use the `marketing-agent` for the plan: YPP preparation and policy compliance, AdSense, affiliates, sponsorships, digital products, memberships. Disclose sponsorships and affiliate links.

### 6. 90-Day Plan
Use the `planner` agent. Structure:
- Weeks 1-2: research, positioning, channel setup, first 3 videos scripted.
- Weeks 3-6: publish on a fixed cadence, test hooks/thumbnails weekly.
- Weeks 7-10: double down on top-performing formats, add Shorts, build playlists.
- Weeks 11-13: review analytics, prepare YPP application and first monetization offers.
Each week lists tasks, output count, and one metric to review.

## Examples

Request: "Help me start a YouTube channel about home lab networking."
1. Run niche/competitor research and return a positioning statement.
2. Propose 3 pillars and 10 video concepts with titles and hooks.
3. Produce the weekly schedule and analytics checklist.

## Guardrails

- No fake engagement, view-botting, or misleading thumbnails; these violate YouTube policy.
- Don't promise monetization dates or income.
- Disclose AI-generated or synthetic content where YouTube requires it.
